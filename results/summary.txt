9. INTERPRETATION
The controller computes the state-feedback dot product using
a BGV ciphertext (fixed-point encoded state) and a plaintext
fixed-point gain vector: packed multiply followed by rotate-and-sum.
Only the resulting control signal is decrypted and rescaled before
it is applied to the simulated plant. BGV evaluates the integer dot
product exactly; the only deviation from the plaintext baseline is
the fixed-point rounding of x and K, which is quantified above.
